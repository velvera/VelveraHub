Order information

Payments and order support

Read the seller, delivery, payment, and total details shown for your order. Verify the available process before sending any payment.

Payment methods

Paystack and Stripe checkout are not available yet. Manual transfers may be used only when the market's receiving account is configured and enabled in checkout.

Order status

Orders record the selected payment method and confirmation status. VelveraHub does not currently offer escrow, trade insurance, or a guaranteed buyer-protection service.

Delivery details

Confirm the delivery area, fee, timing, and fulfilment arrangements with the seller before paying.

Before you order

  • Review the listing, seller, payment instructions, delivery charge, and total.
  • Keep the order number and relevant messages for any support request.
  • Never share passwords, one-time codes, or payment credentials with a seller.

The operator's support contact, refund rules, and dispute process need owner approval before customer orders are accepted.

Privacy

Provide only the account and delivery details required for the current transaction. Review the draft privacy notice and do not share authentication or payment secrets with sellers.

Order terms

Review the seller, delivery coverage, product details, payment instructions, and final total before placing an order. Market-specific consumer terms remain subject to owner and legal review.
