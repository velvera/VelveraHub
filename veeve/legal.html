Nothing excludes rights or liability that cannot legally be excluded.

Governing law, venue, operator identity, and mandatory consumer protections are not yet confirmed. Owner and counsel must complete these terms for each approved market before publication. Account closure does not automatically erase transaction records or complete an account-deletion request.

Refunds and Returns Policy

No return or refund deadline, eligibility rule, seller/platform allocation, or processing time is approved. Remove this draft and publish market-specific rules only after the owner and counsel confirm consumer-law requirements and the payment/refund flow is operational.

Cookie Policy

VelveraHub uses essential browser storage for features such as region, cart, session, and consent. Optional analytics and browsing-based recommendations are enabled only after you accept. Consented activity supports aggregated product, search, checkout, and experiment measurement; the service does not use personal data as an analytics identity.

We may use self-hosted or necessary third-party resources for fonts, icons, authentication, database, payment, and media delivery. Their own policies may apply. You can change your analytics choice by clearing VelveraHub site storage in your browser.

Account deletion and data requests

Account settings currently queue a deletion request; that does not itself delete the Supabase Auth user or complete data erasure. End-to-end deletion, retention exceptions, and confirmation communications have not passed acceptance testing. Do not promise completed deletion until an approved and tested server-side process is in place.
