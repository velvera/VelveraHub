Information the service handles

  • Accounts: email address and authentication data managed by Supabase Auth; profile information may include a name, phone number, region, and seller role. Do not send or store passwords in support requests.
  • Seller applications: shop and business details, service location, contact details, verification status, submitted verification documents, and administrator review records. Verification documents are intended for restricted seller/admin access, not public listings.
  • Listings and communications: product descriptions, prices, stock, category, listing images or videos, reviews, and buyer/seller messages where those features are used.
  • Orders: order items and totals, contact and delivery details supplied at checkout, payment method and status, transaction references, and information needed by the relevant seller to fulfil and support the order. Card numbers are not requested by the current checkout.
  • Service and security events: consented marketplace events and limited diagnostic information such as event type, route, time, region, request reference, and sanitized error details where an event destination is configured. The operator must confirm what is enabled in each deployed environment.
  • Browser storage: essential session/authentication state, region and cart preferences, and the cookie-consent choice. Optional analytics and browsing-based recommendations are intended to remain off unless accepted.

Purposes and legal basis

The service uses account and order information to provide marketplace functions, authenticate users, route orders to sellers, support delivery and customer-service requests, protect the service, moderate listings, and meet legal obligations where applicable. The operator and counsel must document the lawful basis for each purpose in each market; this draft does not select or certify a legal basis.

Who receives information

Relevant sellers receive the buyer and order details needed to fulfil an order. Authorized marketplace administrators can review seller applications, reports, and operational queues. Supabase provides authentication, database, and file storage services; Cloudflare serves the static applications. The browser also loads the Supabase JavaScript client, fonts/icons, and the resumable-upload library from the CDN hosts listed in the owner handoff. Those services may receive ordinary connection metadata such as an IP address and browser headers. Confirm provider contracts, processing locations, and retention before approval.

Current checkout does not implement Paystack or Stripe card processing, and the marketplace does not claim escrow or insurance protection. Manual payment availability depends on the relevant seller and administrator settings; a payment instruction or uploaded receipt is not proof that funds cleared. Do not describe a future provider as a current recipient or service.

International processing and retention

Supabase project location, Cloudflare processing, CDN requests, and any cross-border data transfers must be confirmed by the operator. Retention periods for accounts, seller verification, messages, orders, diagnostics, and legal records have not been approved. The operator and counsel must set a schedule, document lawful exceptions, and ensure backups and private files follow it.

Seller responsibilities and listings

Sellers are responsible for their authority to sell, lawful and accurate product descriptions, authenticity and condition claims, images and other content, stock, displayed prices, applicable taxes, order handling, delivery arrangements, and customer support.
