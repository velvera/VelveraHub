Institution selection is location context for buyers and optional product details; it does not mean a seller is a student or that a listing is for students. All sellers remain subject to administrator approval and verification, and institution-based discovery should remain available.

**Why:** The user clarified that student-specific seller and listing concepts misrepresent VelveraHub; students are served through institution-based location search, while every seller must be approved and verified.

**How to apply:** Do not infer student identity or audience from an institution, and do not restore student-only listing types or labels. Preserve institution location selection, generic marketplace listing flows, administrator approval, and verification.
